Instructions

Before starting the game

  • The app should be used by parents. Your child doesn’t need to operate it just to listen.

  • Parents can use the app with their child for 30 to 60 minutes daily to keep screen time balanced and avoid overuse.

  • Keep volume comfortable for your child, who can wear headphones while using the app.

  • Move to the next stage only after your child learns the words and completes the current stage.

  • Parents can adjust the sound level to help their child learn how words are spoken in different tones of voice.

  • Follow the sounds in the given order to make learning easier for your child.
